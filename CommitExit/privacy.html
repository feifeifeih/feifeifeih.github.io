CommitExit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

CommitExit’s current distributed app keeps commitment records on your device. It has no advertising, analytics SDK, or remote CommitExit session service.

Location and map searches

The app requests your location to help set up a commitment, and monitors the geofence of each active session to evaluate whether you leave or arrive on time. Selected coordinates, radius, deadlines, and timestamped location events may be stored in local session, consent, and audit records. Map display and place search use Apple Maps; search text and map context may be processed by Apple under its policies. Background geofence monitoring applies to active commitments.

Contacts and messages

Names and phone numbers you select, and configured commitment and emergency messages, are stored locally. The contact picker accesses your address book with your permission. CommitExit does not upload your address book or use an SMS provider. You approve sending through Apple Messages, which handles messages with Apple, your carrier, and your chosen recipients. Group messages may expose recipients’ numbers to one another. CommitExit cannot verify delivery or the final contents you edit in Messages.

Consent and audit records

The app stores consent timestamps, app and terms versions, commitment settings, selected contacts, configured messages, authentication results, and session events. These records support local operation and troubleshooting. They are not automatically sent to the developer.

Authentication, notifications, and payments

Apple’s authentication framework performs Face ID, Touch ID, or device-passcode verification. CommitExit receives the result, never biometric templates or your passcode. Local notifications and Live Activities can display commitment details on your device, including its Lock Screen. The distributed app does not process payments or offer financial stakes. Sponsor pairing and CloudKit sharing are disabled.

Sharing and collection

CommitExit does not sell personal data, track you across apps or websites, or share records with advertisers. There is no CommitExit account or remote session database. Apple services, your carrier, and recipients handle information you choose to use with them under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Consent and audit records remain locally until the app’s data is removed; they have no automatic expiry. Active sessions and unresolved failure records are retained for recovery. Delete App removes local app data; Offload App does not. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple backup settings. Deleting CommitExit does not delete previously sent Messages, copies held by recipients, or existing backups.

Your choices

You can change location, contacts, notification, and Live Activity permissions in iPhone Settings. Losing location access may prevent a verified outcome. You choose recipients and approve each message. There is no server account to delete.

Support correspondence and this website

If you contact support, the developer and email provider receive what you send to respond and investigate. Request deletion of support correspondence using the contact below; records may be retained where needed to resolve an ongoing request or meet legal obligations. This website uses no cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.
