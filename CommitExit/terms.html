CommitExit Terms of Use

Last updated: October 7, 2026

CommitExit is a personal accountability tool. By starting a commitment, you agree to the settings and consent shown before activation.

Your commitment

A session begins only after you confirm and Messages reports Send for your prefilled plan. There is no ordinary cancel after activation. You can use the emergency control to end a session and prepare an emergency message. All messages require your approval in Apple Messages.

Limitations

Location, background execution, notifications, and message delivery depend on your device and service providers. Results can be uncertain. CommitExit is not an emergency service or a guarantee that someone will check on you. Use local emergency services when needed.

Respect your contacts

Choose people who agree to receive your accountability messages. Review recipients and contents before sending, and consider that group conversations can reveal phone numbers.

Current release

The distributed app offers no financial stakes or payment processing. Apple’s standard app license agreement applies where applicable. See the privacy policy for data handling.
