CommitExit Support

Help with commitments, permissions, and messages.

Contact support

feifeifeih@gmail.com

Include your iOS version, CommitExit version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please do not send contact lists, precise locations, message contents, or other sensitive information unless needed to investigate your request.

Start a commitment

Choose Leave in time or Be there on time, set your location and deadline, and choose trusted contacts. Review the commitment, confirm with device authentication, and tap Send in Apple Messages. Canceling or failing to send does not start the session. The announced deadline does not move while you compose.

Location and background operation

In iPhone Settings, find CommitExit and allow Always location access for background geofence events. Enable Precise Location for more accurate boundaries. GPS, system scheduling, and device power can affect results. If the outcome cannot be verified, the app marks it as needing review.

Messages are never automatic

Every message requires your Send tap in Apple Messages. On success, use Send completion update. On verified failure, the result remains pending until Messages reports Send. This reports your action, not confirmed delivery. Friends do not need the app. Multiple recipients may see each other’s phone numbers in a group conversation.

Cannot send or finish a failed session?

Check that Messages is configured and available on your iPhone. Reopen CommitExit to retry the pending message. A simulator cannot test actual SMS delivery. If the composer still cannot send, contact support.

Notifications and Live Activities

Allow notifications in Settings for deadline reminders. Live Activity availability depends on your device and system settings. A missing notification does not mean your commitment was completed.

Stop because you feel unsafe

Use the emergency control to end the session and prepare a message to your contacts, then tap Send. CommitExit does not contact emergency services and is not an emergency-response service.

Payments and accounts

The distributed app has no financial stakes, payment processing, or CommitExit account. Development builds contain simulated payment features.

Delete local data

Delete CommitExit using Delete App in iPhone Settings to remove its local data; Offload App keeps documents and data. Messages already sent, recipient copies, and existing device backups are managed separately. See the privacy policy.
